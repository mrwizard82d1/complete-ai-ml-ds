Why NumPy?
- Very popular
- Similar to Python `lists`

Two main reasons
- Many `NumPy` functions written in C
	- Operations are **much, much** faster in `NumPy`
- Machines understand 1's and 0's
	- `NumPy` allows the data analyst to use **numbers** to learn
	- A `NumPy array` is much easier to work with than other structures
