A quick tip from Andre
- Clean
- Transform
- Reduce

A step back: why do we do the things we do?
- More data not better in and of itself
- Strive for **useful** data

The clean-transform-reduce is a prerequisite to effective analysis

Why a clean data?
- Remove and replace data
	- For example, missing data
	- May remove a missing row
	- Replace missinga data

Why transform data?
-  Computers **don't** understand concepts
	- Must be "encoded"
- Will see Daniel transform non-numeric information into numbers
- For example, do you have heart diseases
	- Yes and no become 1 and 0
- Comes with **practice**

Why reduce data?
- More data "costs more money"
	- Can get same result on less data => save money
- AKA
	- Dimensionality reduction
	- Column reduction

Part of our skill is to transform
- The data we have
- Into data that is **more** useful (to our analysis)
